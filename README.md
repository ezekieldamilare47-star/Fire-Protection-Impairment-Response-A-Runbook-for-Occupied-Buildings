# Fire Protection Impairment Response: A Runbook for Occupied Buildings

## Purpose

This runbook defines how facility teams in occupied buildings (hospitals, senior living, hotels, residential towers, schools, and offices) should respond when a fire alarm or sprinkler system is impaired. It covers detection, decision points, fire watch deployment, and closeout, and it includes a vendor evaluation section for choosing among the best fire watch companies near me.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Impairment | Any condition where a fire alarm or sprinkler system, or part of it, is out of service |
| Planned impairment | Scheduled shutdown for repair, upgrade, or testing |
| Emergency impairment | Unplanned failure, damage, or fault |
| AHJ | Authority having jurisdiction, typically the fire marshal |
| Fire watch | Trained personnel patrolling impaired areas to detect fire and initiate response |
| ILSM | Interim life safety measures, used in healthcare during deficiencies or construction |

## Thresholds (Commonly Cited)

| System | Out-of-Service Threshold | Required Action |
|---|---|---|
| Fire alarm | > 4 hours in a 24-hour period | Evacuate or approved fire watch |
| Sprinkler | > 10 hours in a 24-hour period | Evacuate or approved fire watch |

Sources commonly cited: NFPA 101 (Life Safety Code), IFC 901.7, NFPA 25 (sprinkler impairments), NFPA 72 (fire alarm systems). Local amendments and the AHJ control the final requirement. Healthcare facilities may also be subject to accreditation and federal program expectations.

## Runbook

### Phase 1: Detect and Confirm (T+0)
- [ ] Confirm the impairment and affected zones
- [ ] Record start time (this starts the threshold clock)
- [ ] Notify the fire department, monitoring company, and insurer as procedures require
- [ ] Tag the impaired system and control valves

### Phase 2: Decide (T+0 to T+30 min)
- [ ] Estimate repair time
- [ ] If repair may exceed thresholds, request fire watch immediately
- [ ] Confirm with the AHJ whether fire watch is accepted or evacuation is required
- [ ] Identify vulnerable occupants (patients, mobility-impaired residents, sleeping guests)

### Phase 3: Deploy Fire Watch
- [ ] Provide site address, access instructions, affected areas, floor plans
- [ ] Provide evacuation plan and staff contacts for each shift
- [ ] Confirm patrol interval required by the AHJ
- [ ] Confirm guard arrival and start of log

### Phase 4: Operate
- [ ] Guard patrols all impaired areas at the required interval
- [ ] Guard coordinates with front desk, nursing station, or maintenance
- [ ] Every round logged with time, areas, observations, actions
- [ ] Shift handoffs logged with no gap

### Phase 5: Restore and Release
- [ ] System repaired and tested as required
- [ ] Notifications closed out
- [ ] AHJ releases the fire watch
- [ ] Final log and records delivered to facility management
